# Simply Bookkeeping

**Simple, vibe-coded, self-hosted bookkeeping for Australian small businesses.**

Simply Bookkeeping is for businesses that want straightforward income and expense tracking without the complexity and ongoing cost of a full accounting platform.

There's no Payroll, GST, BAS or any other bloat which many small businesses don't require.

## 

## Income \& Sales Tracking

* Record income with itemised, categorised line items
* Supports positive and negative amounts for adjustments, fees and discounts
* Track full or partial payments and balances due
* Create quotes, output to PDF and email directly to the contact

### 

### Expense Tracking

* Record itemised business expenses
* Track payments and outstanding balances
* Attach invoices, receipts and other documentation
* Use the Uploads holding area to keep documents ready to be matched to expense records
* OCR will extract information from uploaded invoices in the holding area, and data-match it in a newly created income record (note that the success of the OCR module is dependent on the layout of the supplier's invoice)

### 

### Invoices

Generate a customer-ready PDF invoice directly from an income record. Email it directly to them from the app.

Invoices can include your business logo/branding, contact information and payment details.

### 

### Profit \& Loss Report

View financial performance broken down by category.

Reports can be viewed in the application, filtered by financial year or custom date range, and exported as PDF.

### 

### Customers \& Suppliers

Maintain a contacts database for individuals and companies, with linked financial records.

### 

### Multiple Businesses

Manage multiple businesses from one Simply Bookkeeping installation. Each business has its own financial records, contacts, categories, attachments, branding and payment details.
Multiple user accounts are supported, with granular access to the businesses you choose. Read only accountant logons are also supported.
